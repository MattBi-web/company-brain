# Port Congestion Advisory — Rotterdam & Long Beach (Example, Week 41)

**Status:** Active advisory. **Scope:** affects demurrage/detention free-time clocks and intermodal SLA
timers (see `demurrage_detention_policy.md` and `intermodal_sla_policy.md`) for the two ports below.

## Rotterdam (Europoort / Maasvlakte terminals)
- Cause: a multi-day equipment-handling backlog following a labor action at two of the four container
  terminals.
- Expected impact: +3 to +5 days on yard dwell time before a container becomes retrievable by truck.
- Meridian action: automatic free-time extension requests filed with Maersk, MSC, and Hapag-Lloyd for all
  active Rotterdam imports. Confirmations logged per booking, not blanket-assumed.
- Customer guidance: do not schedule warehouse appointments assuming standard transit — check the
  booking's live milestone feed before committing a delivery slot.

## Long Beach
- Cause: vessel bunching after an upstream schedule recovery from a prior Pacific storm system; three
  vessels discharging within a 36-hour window at adjacent berths.
- Expected impact: +2 to +3 days on rail ramp availability for the Chicago and Dallas corridors.
- Meridian action: intermodal SLA clock for affected bookings is paused for the advisory window per
  policy (SLA exclusions section); ops has flagged all in-flight Long Beach → Chicago/Dallas moves.

## How this interacts with policy documents
- The advisory window is **excluded**, not just extended, from the intermodal SLA transit clock.
- Demurrage/detention free-time **extensions are requested** per the standard carrier process — they are
  not automatic contractual entitlements, and confirmation varies by carrier and by individual booking.

## Review cadence
Advisories are reviewed every 48 hours by Ops and either renewed, narrowed, or closed. This document
reflects the state at time of writing and should not be treated as permanently current — always check
the live advisory board for the actual status before quoting a customer.
