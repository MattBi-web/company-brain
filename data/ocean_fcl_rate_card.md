## Surcharges (not included above — applied per carrier's published index)
- **BAF (Bunker Adjustment Factor):** varies monthly with fuel index, quoted separately per booking.
- **PSS (Peak Season Surcharge):** typically applied August–October on Transpacific lanes.
- **Low Sulphur Surcharge (LSS):** applies fleet-wide per IMO 2020 fuel compliance.
- **Panama Canal / Suez Canal transit fee:** applies only to routings through the respective canal.

## How to use this document
Treat every figure here as a **starting reference point for a conversation**, not a quotable rate.
Confirm against the live rate engine before quoting a customer — rates move weekly on most Transpacific
lanes during peak season.
