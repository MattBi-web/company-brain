# Carrier Contract Summary — Maersk (Example / Illustrative)

**Note:** this is a simplified, illustrative summary for the Company Brain demo dataset — figures are
invented for demonstration and do not represent a real commercial agreement.

## Scope
Service Contract covering Transpacific (Asia → US West Coast) and Asia → North Europe lanes, FCL only,
dry and reefer equipment.

## Free time terms (overrides the general policy where more favorable)
- Transpacific dry: **5 days demurrage / 6 days detention** (one day better than Meridian's general
  policy in `demurrage_detention_policy.md` — negotiated volume tier).
- Transpacific reefer: **3 days demurrage / 4 days detention**.
- Asia → North Europe: standard policy terms apply (7/7), no override.

## Combined free time
This contract uses a **combined free-time pool** on Transpacific lanes only: 11 days combined (dry),
7 days combined (reefer) — not split demurrage+detention. North Europe lanes remain split (standard
policy).

## Minimum volume commitment
Indicative minimum quarterly volume commitment with a rate review if utilization falls below 80% of
commitment for two consecutive quarters. (Illustrative figure only — not a real tier.)

## Rate validity
Rates locked for the contract period subject to the carrier's standard BAF (Bunker Adjustment Factor)
and PSS (Peak Season Surcharge) pass-through clauses, applied per the carrier's published index, not
negotiated per shipment.

## Claims handling
Cargo claims follow the Hague-Visby Rules package limitation unless a higher value is declared and the
applicable ad valorem freight is paid at booking.
