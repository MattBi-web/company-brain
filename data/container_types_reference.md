# Container Types — Reference Sheet (Meridian Intermodal Logistics)

## Dry Van (General Purpose)
- **20ft (20GP):** ~33.2 m³ capacity, ~28,200 kg max payload.
- **40ft (40GP):** ~67.6 m³ capacity, ~26,700 kg max payload.
- **40ft High Cube (40HC):** ~76.3 m³ capacity, ~26,500 kg max payload. Extra 1 foot of internal height —
  do not use for cargo requiring low-clearance European inland transport without checking route clearance.

## Reefer (Refrigerated)
- **20ft Reefer:** temperature range -30°C to +30°C, requires continuous power (genset or plug) through
  every transport leg including port stacking — a gap in power coverage is the top cause of reefer claims.
- **40ft Reefer:** same range, larger capacity; standard for produce and pharma lanes.
- Reefer bookings must specify set-point temperature, humidity control (if needed), and ventilation
  setting at time of booking — carriers will not accept late changes within 48h of vessel cut-off.

## Open Top
For over-height cargo loaded from above (e.g. machinery). Requires a tarpaulin and is generally not
stackable — always confirm with the carrier whether open-top units get a non-stack premium on the lane.

## Flat Rack
For oversized/out-of-gauge cargo (e.g. vehicles, large machinery, steel coils). Collapsible or
fixed-end versions available; out-of-gauge dimensions must be declared pre-booking for vessel stowage
planning, or the carrier can refuse loading at the terminal.

## Tank Container
For bulk liquids/chemicals. Requires UN/IMO classification on file before booking; hazardous cargo
surcharges apply per IMO class, not per container type.

## Quick payload comparison
| Type | Internal volume | Typical max payload |
|---|---|---|
| 20GP | ~33.2 m³ | ~28,200 kg |
| 40GP | ~67.6 m³ | ~26,700 kg |
| 40HC | ~76.3 m³ | ~26,500 kg |
| 20 Reefer | ~28.3 m³ | ~27,400 kg |
| 40 Reefer | ~59.3 m³ | ~27,700 kg |

Figures are manufacturer-typical and vary by container age/make — always use the actual tare weight on
the container's CSC plate for stowage and weight-declaration (VGM) purposes, never the table above.
